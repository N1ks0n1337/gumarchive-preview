INTRODUCTION

When the wrapper outlived the product

The gum disappeared, but its wrappers remained. This collection brings together commercial branding, serial inserts and the physical traces of family preservation. American packaging, Soviet Olympic graphic design, East German comics, Turkish car cards and Yugoslav animated characters coexist in one album.
